How to Recycle Plastic Grocery Bags

  • Step 1: Keep out of blue bins. Never put loose plastic bags or wraps into your curbside recycling container. They act as "tanglers," wrapping around automated sorting gear and shutting down operations.
  • Step 2: Empty out entirely. Remove all receipts, loose change, food crumbs, and plastic tags inside the bags.
  • Step 3: Ensure they are dry. Make sure the bags are perfectly clean and dry; moist bags can ruin an entire collection pile.
  • Step 4: Pack together. Stuff many small plastic bags inside one larger bag to keep them tightly compressed before bringing them to the storefront bin.
What else fits? Store drop-off bins generally accept other clean, stretchy plastic wraps too! This includes case overwraps (from water bottles), bubble wrap, air pillows, and clean bread bags.
