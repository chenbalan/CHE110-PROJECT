How to Properly Recycle Your Cardboard Boxes

  • Step 1: Empty completely. Remove all plastic wrap, packing peanuts, bubble wrap, and styrofoam inserts.
  • Step 2: Remove excess tape. Pull off large strips of heavy plastic shipping tape or shipping labels if required locally.
  • Step 3: Flatten entirely. Break down the boxes flat by cutting or opening the bottom tape seams to save sorting truck space.
  • Step 4: Keep it dry. Make sure the cardboard does not get wet from grease or rain, as wet fibers cannot be processed.
Why flatten? Unflattened boxes take up immense amounts of space in recycling trucks and sorting bins, making collection inefficient and messy.
