How to Properly Compost Your Food Scraps

  • Step 1: Separate correctly. Collect fruit peels, vegetable scraps, coffee grounds, and eggshells.
  • Step 2: Know what to exclude. Keep out meat, dairy, bones, fats, and oils unless your city specifically notes they accept them in high-heat industrial systems.
  • Step 3: Remove stickers and ties. Always peel off plastic price stickers from fruit skins and remove rubber bands.
  • Step 4: Use a breathable container. Store your scraps in a small countertop bin lined with a certified compostable bag or freeze them to eliminate odors entirely before disposal.
Why compost food? When food decays in a sealed landfill, it produces harmful methane gas. Composting allows it to break down cleanly with oxygen, turning waste into nutrient-rich soil.
