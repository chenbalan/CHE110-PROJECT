How to Properly Dispose of Clothing & Textiles

  • Step 1: Assess item condition. Clean, wearable garments should be set aside for clothing banks, shelters, or thrift store donations to extend their lifecycle.
  • Step 2: Prepare unwearable fabrics. Torn, stained, or heavily worn items (such as old sheets, towels, or rags) should still be saved for designated textile recycling systems rather than thrown in the trash.
  • Step 3: Wash and dry thoroughly. All textiles must be completely clean and dry before collection. Damp fabrics can grow mold, ruining entire sorting piles.
  • Step 4: Pack and secure collections. Place garments inside robust bags to protect them from weather and moisture damage inside outdoor drop-off dropboxes.
Why specialized tracking? Up to 95% of household textile materials can be recycled, repurposed, or downcycled into industrial insulation, carpet padding, and wiping cloths, avoiding massive landfill waste.
