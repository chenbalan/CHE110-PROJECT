How to Safely Handle Electrical & E-Waste

  • Step 1: Separate items. Keep old appliances, broken chords, adapters, and electronic devices completely isolated from standard recyclables.
  • Step 2: Wipe personal data. Ensure smartphones, computers, or storage devices are completely wiped clean of personal data before drops.
  • Step 3: Remove detachable batteries. If the electrical device has an easily removable battery (especially lithium-ion), remove it and place it in a designated battery drop-off bucket.
  • Step 4: Keep hardware intact. Avoid breaking screens, cracking cases, or exposing internal wiring, as broken e-waste can leak toxins.
Why specialized tracking? Electronics contain toxic elements like lead, cadmium, and mercury alongside valuable metals. Drop-off systems keep chemicals out of local groundwater tables while recovering precious materials.
