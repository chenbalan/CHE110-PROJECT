How to Properly Compost Shredded Paper & Napkins

  • Step 1: Verify the paper type. Only compost matte, non-glossy plain printer paper, clean napkins, and unbleached paper towels. Avoid glossy inserts or colored inks.
  • Step 2: Watch out for hidden plastics. Ensure napkins do not contain synthetic fibers or plastic decorations, and verify that envelopes do not have plastic address windows.
  • Step 3: Check for heavy chemical contamination. Napkins or towels soaked in heavy industrial grease, cleaning chemicals, or motor oil must go in the regular trash.
  • Step 4: Scatter and aerate. Shredded paper clumps easily when wet, blocking oxygen paths. Scatter it lightly across your bin and mix it well with damp vegetative kitchen waste.
Carbon Booster Tip: Shredded paper and plain napkins act as excellent carbon-rich ("brown") materials. They absorb excess moisture from vegetable scraps and keep home compost bins structurally balanced and odor-free!
