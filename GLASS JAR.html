How to Properly Recycle Your Glass Jars

  • Step 1: Empty and rinse. Wash out any food remnants like sauce or jam to avoid contaminating other recyclables.
  • Step 2: Separate the lids. Metal lids can usually be recycled, but they should be removed from the jar first. Plastic lids typically go in the trash.
  • Step 3: Keep labels on. You don't need to scrub off paper labels; they burn off during the high-heat recycling process.
  • Step 4: Avoid mixing glass types. Only recycle food and beverage containers. Never mix in window glass, mirrors, or Pyrex glassware, as they melt at different temperatures.
Why recycle glass? Glass is 100% recyclable and can be repurposed endlessly without loss in purity or quality. Using recycled glass also reduces energy consumption and carbon emissions during manufacturing.
