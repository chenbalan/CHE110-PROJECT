Expanded polystyrene (Styrofoam) is rarely recycled curbside. Enter your ZIP code to verify local drop-off programs or ban policies.

How to Properly Dispose of Styrofoam Cups

  • Step 1: Check curbside limits. Do not place Styrofoam items into your regular curbside recycling bin; it breaks into micro-fragments that ruin other recyclables.
  • Step 2: Empty food residue. Toss any remaining liquids, straws, or plastic lids into the appropriate bins.
  • Step 3: Secure lightweight pieces. Bag lightweight foam products tightly if placing them in outdoor trash receptacles to prevent wind blowaways.
  • Step 4: Seek alternative drop-offs. Look up specialized local environmental centers that use specialized densifier machines to handle bulk foam recycling.
The Styrofoam Problem: Expanded polystyrene takes hundreds of years to break down in a landfill. Try switching to reusable tumblers or biodegradable paper cups wherever possible!
