How Can We Help?

The waste that we create has to be carefully controlled to ensure it does not harm our environment and public health. Adopting smart recycling habits is a simple yet powerful step toward a sustainable future.

Core Principles

What is Garbage?

Unwanted or discarded materials resulting from human activities that require proper segregation and disposal management.

Reduce, Reuse & Recycle

Reduce consumption, Reuse items creatively, and Recycle materials to give them a second life.

Community & Action Plan

  • Kids Club Initiatives: Engaging youth in environmental awareness.
    1. Weekly Eco-Activities and clean-up drives.
    2. Interactive learning via Recycle City models.
  • Proper Material Sorting: Separating paper, plastics, glass, and organic waste.
