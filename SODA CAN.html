How to Properly Recycle Your Soda Cans

  • Step 1: Empty completely. Drain any leftover soda, juice, or liquid contents.
  • Step 2: Quick rinse. Give it a light rinse with water to remove sticky residues and avoid drawing bugs.
  • Step 3: Leave the tab on. Keep the aluminum pull-tab attached to the can so it doesn't get lost in sorting machines.
  • Step 4: Keep it uncrushed (usually). Most modern automated sorting systems identify cans by their 3D shape, so check if your city prefers them unflattened.
Why recycle aluminum? Aluminum can be recycled endlessly without losing its quality. Making a brand new can from recycled material uses 95% less energy than producing one from raw ore!
